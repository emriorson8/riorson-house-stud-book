How it works

  1. Add your horses. Fill in the stat sheet just like the in-game screen and drop in a photo or screenshot.
  2. Record a breeding when a mare is covered. Pick her and the stallion, and a live pregnancy timer starts.
  3. When the timer ends, the foal is added to the Foals tab by itself with its dam and sire. You can also tap Foal born to add it early.
  4. Each foal gets a grow-up timer. When it ends, the foal moves to Horses by itself.
  5. Open any horse to see its bloodline, its foals and every breeding it has been part of.
  6. Selling a horse? Open it and tap List for sale for a private sale, or Send to auction. The Sales & auctions tab keeps the two lists apart. When it goes, tick Sold and add the buyer and price.
  7. Foals have their own tab. Tick Grown up when one becomes an adult and it moves to Horses.
  8. Stat settings holds the personality, metabolism, handling and breed choices. Change them to match your server.
